# Global Mining Network FAQ

## How do rewards work?
Rewards are server-authoritative and ledger-backed. Your effective hashrate contributes work to the one global chain and rewards are settled by the server.

## Why did my effective hashrate change?
Power and cooling multipliers are applied server-side. If either subsystem is constrained, your effective output may drop.

## Can I recover beta progress?
Eligible beta accounts can migrate to production with progression and inventory preserved, while balance transfers apply a 10% migration tax.
